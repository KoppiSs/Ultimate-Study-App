# Retrieval Practice

Close your notes and write down what you remember about a topic. Then check your answer against the notes and correct anything you missed.

## Five-minute exercise

1. Pick a topic from your last study session.
2. Write three questions about it from memory.
3. Answer each question without looking at your notes.
4. Check your answers and schedule another review for the hardest question.
